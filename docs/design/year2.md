# Year 2 question level

Teachers assign Year 2 or Year 6 in the class register. Unassigned pupils default to Year 6; previous pupil-selected levels are ignored. Assignment persists on the player's database record and is enforced for new questions, answers and mistake retries. Pupils cannot change it. All pupils remain in the same world. Existing histories and rewards remain intact, and switching levels does not reset question history or the 24-hour retry rule.

167 original starter questions: 76 maths, 36 English (including four short reading passages), 25 verbal reasoning and 30 non-verbal reasoning. Year 2 rewards are explicitly 10 coins for quick questions and 20 for reading or longer problems. The bank combines typed responses and multiple choice.

Maths scope includes two-digit place value, addition/subtraction, 2/5 multiplication and sharing, fractions, money, time, shapes, measures and simple data. English includes spelling, plurals, grammar, punctuation, retrieval and simple inference. Reasoning puzzles are age-appropriate enrichment, not separate statutory Year 2 subjects.

Each submitted answer counts towards a persisted 10-per-subject round, regardless of correctness. Question skips do not count. All four subjects must reach 10 before counters reset; fully mastered subjects are excluded to avoid a deadlock. Correct answers never repeat, including after a round reset or year change. Unresolved mistakes remain eligible after 24 hours and obey the same limits. Changing year invalidates pending questions while retaining history and round progress.
