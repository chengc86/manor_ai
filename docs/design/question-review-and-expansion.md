# Question review and original expansion

## Review scope
Reviewed the currently accessible topic lists and one question example in each of English, verbal reasoning, maths and non-verbal reasoning in the signed-in Atom Learning student UI. This was a limited review, not an exhaustive audit of its question bank. No answers or skips were submitted. Further questions and chapters were behind activity progression. No source questions, passages or artwork were imported into Manor Quest.

Visible topics included nouns, verbs, main clauses, punctuation, fiction and prefixes; synonyms, antonyms and definitions; ordering/comparing, rounding, negative numbers, Roman numerals and addition; visual classification, matching, pairing and sequences. Observed formats included single-choice questions, word-pair selection, diagram options and optional question help. These inform broad topic coverage, not reproduction of source questions.

Source reviewed through its normal UI: https://app.atomlearning.com/student/

## Original question set
400 new Year 6 questions, 100 per subject:
- Maths: 10 each on ordering, rounding, negative numbers, Roman numerals, mental addition, written addition, multiplication word problems, fractions, perimeter and percentage discounts.
- English: 50 grammar questions and 50 comprehension questions using 10 original passages. Includes retrieval, inference, vocabulary in context, authorial effect and summaries.
- Verbal reasoning: synonyms, antonyms, definitions and letter codes, 25 each.
- Non-verbal reasoning: rotation, reflection, classification, visual analogies and sequences, 20 each, with 100 original diagrams.

Answer choices are deterministically shuffled. Rewards follow the existing 20/30/40 tiers in this set. Existing IDs, histories, teacher-assigned year groups and subject limits are unchanged. The Year 2 bank remains separate.

## Follow-up expansion: 240 questions

A further targeted UI review covered an authorial-effect fiction question, an antonym-pair question, a negative-number word problem and visual-reasoning guidance. Activities were opened for inspection; no answers or skips were submitted. This remains a sample-based review, not an exhaustive examination of Atom's bank. No question wording, passages or artwork were imported.

Added 160 Year 6 questions (40 per subject) and 80 Year 2 questions (20 per subject). The combined bank now has 2,436 questions: 2,109 Year 6 and 327 Year 2.

- Year 6 maths: ratio, equations, cuboid volume, angles, fraction calculations, elapsed time, means, discounts/change, coordinates and common multiples.
- Year 6 English: six original passages with retrieval, inference, vocabulary, authorial effect and purpose; grammar and punctuation.
- Year 6 reasoning: contextual vocabulary, antonyms, analogies, codes, grid overlays, subtraction and ordered transformations.
- Year 2: practical arithmetic/money, three short original stories, basic grammar, vocabulary, classification and simpler visual movement/reflection puzzles.

All 240 questions have explanations and explicit coin reward tiers. There are 42 comprehension questions across nine original passages and 60 original SVG puzzles. Visual distractors use similar patterns (and equal shaded-square counts for transformations) so appearance alone does not identify the correct answer.

Validation includes duplicate checks against the existing bank, balanced year/subject counts, independent arithmetic/code checks for 70 answers, independent reconstruction of all 60 SVG answer keys, the full-bank quality checks and existing persistence/year-isolation tests. Existing question IDs and history are retained; teacher-assigned year groups continue to control which bank a child receives.
