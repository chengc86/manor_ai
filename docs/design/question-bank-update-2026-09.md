# English question bank expansion

Added 80 original English questions: 60 reading questions across 12 texts, plus 20 grammar/vocabulary questions. Total 1,397: Maths 935, English 133, Verbal reasoning 173, Non-verbal reasoning 156.

Reading includes narrative, poetry, a formal letter, diary, information notice, non-fiction report and paired viewpoints. Skills: retrieval, inference supported by evidence, vocabulary in context, summary, authorial language and structure, comparison, and fact versus opinion. Short exact answers are used for retrieval; interpretive questions use multiple choice to avoid rejecting valid free-form explanations through exact matching. Each question includes feedback. Texts are original fiction or illustrative examples, not factual claims about Manor's activities.

Curriculum reference: Department for Education, upper KS2 English programme of study (Years 5–6), checked 15 September 2026:
https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-english-programmes-of-study/national-curriculum-in-england-english-programmes-of-study

Old question IDs and content remain unchanged; new questions have stable reading-v2 / english-v2 IDs. Existing correct-answer history and wrong-answer cooldown apply unchanged. This is a supplementary bank, not an official SATs assessment. Longer teacher-reviewed reading and writing tasks are still useful alongside these automatically marked questions.
