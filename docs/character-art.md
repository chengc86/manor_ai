# Character art revision

Generated using built-in Image Gen, September 2026. Game art is in public/uniforms, public/accessories and public/weapons. These are static sprites with accessory overlays, not rigged skeletal animation assets.

Every hero stands UPRIGHT on EXACTLY TWO LEGS and has EXACTLY TWO ARMS ending in clear hand-like paws. Owl must have two usable hand-like feathered arms, dragon two arms plus small wings behind. All same proportions: large expressive head, compact torso, visible waist, two separate hands at hips, two feet. Same front-facing slight 3/4 pose, arms slightly away from body to allow clothing layers. Polished high quality stylized 3D storybook game render, soft fur, charming brave faces, gold warm highlights, coherent lighting upper left. Each character centered in its cell with ample transparent margins, feet same baseline, head same height except ears/horns. No quadrupeds, no animal on four legs, no text, no labels, no dividers, no ground or shadows outside characters. Aspect ratio 2:1.

Cutout edit prompt: Remove ALL background and all coloured glows from this sprite sheet. Keep the six objects exactly in their existing locations and shapes. Output true transparent RGBA alpha, completely transparent outside objects including inside necklace and between earrings. No dark background, no checkerboard drawn into pixels. Preserve fine golden necklace chain and soft silk edges. This is an asset cutout task, not a redesign.

## Integrated release

14 upright animal heroes, each with boy/girl winter, summer and sports variants (84 sprites). SHUS includes uniform purchases and seven accessories. The battle uses generated school-themed weapon icons and a Manor-inspired campus background. All artwork is a stylised game adaptation, not an official school illustration.
